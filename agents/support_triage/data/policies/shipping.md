# Shipping and delivery

Orders are processed within 2 business days, then shipped by UPS or USPS. Customers receive a tracking link by email when the order ships.

If an order shows as shipped, share the carrier and the estimated delivery time. If a shipped order is more than 5 days late, open a ticket with the shipping team at high priority.

Orders that are still processing can be cancelled free of charge. Orders that have shipped cannot be cancelled, but can be returned after delivery.
